Privacy policy

Hub Labeller is a personal tool used only by its developer, on the developer's own Gmail accounts.

  • It reads message metadata (such as sender, subject and authentication headers) to decide which label to add, and adds labels. It does not send, delete or forward mail.
  • It stores message IDs and the labels it added. For review, it may keep a message's sender and subject for about 30 days. This data is stored encrypted in the developer's own AWS account.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train general AI models.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's "Third-party apps with account access" settings.
